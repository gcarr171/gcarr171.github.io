About

Hellooo, welcome to my website.

My name is Geritza. I'm a photographer and set designer based in Brooklyn. Originally from Maryland, I studied English, Linguistics, and Film & Media Studies at Georgetown University. My professional background includes book publishing and magazine design. I'm currently earning my MLIS and Master of History of Art and Design degrees at Pratt Institute. I enjoy yoga, live music, and going to the movies.

For my INFO 654 Web Basics Project, I chose to make a creative portfolio showcasing my work in photography and set design.

I really enjoyed how challenging and rewarding this experience turned out to be. Not only did I gain a practical foundation of HTML and CSS skills, but I was also pushed to publish a professional site that I've been procrastinating for years.

Thanks for visiting :)
